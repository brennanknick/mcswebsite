How it works

Played with a bow

The ball is a spectral arrow.

Hold the arrow and you have the ball, glowing in your team's colour so everyone can see you through walls. Shoot to pass or to score: hit a teammate and it's a pass, hit an opponent and it's theirs. Miss, and the ball rests on the grass until someone runs over it.

How to play

A referee that never blinks

Real rules, called automatically.

Kickoffs go backward. Goals only count from beyond halfway and outside the box, and if yours doesn't, a NO GOAL sign over the net tells you why. Corners, pass-ins and goal kicks are called the moment the ball settles.

See every rule

Walk in. Kick off.

No queue, and no waiting for staff.

Step into a pitch's ready zone and a hologram counts who's in. Fill it and the countdown starts: rising chimes, 3, 2, 1, then GO! and fireworks in the team colours. Stand in the half you want to defend and that's your side.

Seven positions

Goalkeeper, centre back, left and right defence, left and right wing, and forward, each with its own spot on the pitch. Pick yours from a menu laid out like the field. Skip it and you're handed a free one at kickoff.

Every goal counts

Your career follows you from match to match. /stats shows your record, the best player on the pitch is named MVP at full time, and the leaderboards at spawn track MVPs, clean sheets and more, updating themselves.

Connect to the server

  1. Open Minecraft: Java Edition and go to Multiplayer.
  2. Click Add Server and enter
  3. Join, and accept the resource pack. It carries the kits, crests and goals, so the server needs it.
  4. Walk into a pitch's ready zone and the match starts itself.
