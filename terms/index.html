These terms

By joining the server, visiting this site, using our Discord bot or buying from the store, you agree to these terms and our Privacy Policy. If you don't agree, please don't use MCS.

"MCS", "we" and "us" mean the small team that runs it.

Who can play

  • You need to be 13 or older, the same as Discord.
  • You need your own Minecraft: Java Edition account. You're responsible for everything done on it, so don't share it.
  • To buy something, you need to be old enough to accept PayNow's Buyer Agreement, or have a parent or guardian buy it for you.

Playing fair

Follow the match rules, the rules in game (/rules) and our Discord rules. Don't:

  • Cheat, use hacked clients or exploit bugs.
  • Use alt accounts to dodge a ban or to boost stats, ratings or Elo.
  • Harass, threaten or spam other players.
  • Post anything hateful, sexual or illegal.
  • Pretend to be staff.

When someone breaks the rules, staff can mute, kick or ban them, reset their stats or remove items. Staff decide what counts as breaking them. A ban doesn't come with a refund.

Buying things

How it works

The store sells through PayNow, which is the seller for every order. PayNow's Buyer Agreement covers payment, tax and refunds.

Your order goes to the Minecraft account you bought it for, usually within a minute. If you're offline, it waits until you next join.

Sapphires

Sapphires are our in-game currency. They only work on MCS, they have no cash value, and they can't be cashed out, sold or traded. You can buy them for a friend as a gift.

What you get

  • Everything you can buy is cosmetic or a lobby perk. None of it gives you an edge in a match.
  • Some items last for a set time, shown before you buy.
  • Crates give a random cosmetic. Every crate's contents and exact odds are on the crate odds page.

Gifts

A gift goes to exactly the name you type, so check the spelling. A gift sent to the wrong name can't always be moved.

Refunds and chargebacks

  • Refunds are handled by PayNow, under its Buyer Agreement.
  • If you bought from the EU, you can withdraw within 14 days of buying and get your money back for any Sapphires you haven't used.
  • Refunded items and Sapphires are taken back off your account.
  • If you charge a payment back through your bank, we can remove what it bought and suspend the account until it's settled.

Changes to items

We can change, rebalance, rename or retire items, prices and features. If MCS ever closes, Sapphires and items end with it. None of this comes with a refund, except where the law says otherwise.

What you post

You keep what you make: your chat, names, skins, messages and reports. You let us show and use it to run MCS, like your name in a match report or your messages in chat. Only post things you have the right to share.

The AI characters

Gaffer Pete and Kit Man Kev are AI. They can get things wrong, and what they say isn't a staff decision. Don't tell them anything private: the Privacy Policy says where their chats go.

The server as it is

MCS is provided "as is". As far as the law allows, we aren't responsible for lost progress, items or time, or for what other players do. If we're ever responsible for a loss, it's limited to what you paid in the 12 months before.

Ending things

You can stop playing any time. We can suspend or end your access if you break these terms, without a refund, except where the law says otherwise.

Connect to the server

  1. Open Minecraft: Java Edition 1.21.4 or newer and go to Multiplayer.
  2. Click Add Server and enter
  3. Join, and accept the resource pack. It carries the kits, crests and goals, so the server needs it.
  4. Walk into a pitch's ready zone and the match starts itself.
