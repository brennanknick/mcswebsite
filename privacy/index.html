Privacy Policy

What MCS collects when you play, visit this site or use our Discord bot, why we collect it, and what you can ask us to do with it.

Last updated 27 October 2026

The short version

  • We collect what it takes to run the server: your Minecraft name and ID, your IP address, your chat and what happens in your matches.
  • Your name and match stats are public, on this site and in our Discord.
  • No ads, no tracking cookies, and we never sell your data.
  • PayNow handles payments, so we never see your card.
  • Email us any time to see or delete your data.

Who we are

MCS is a Minecraft soccer server run by a small team. In this policy, "MCS", "we" and "us" mean that team. It covers the server at play.mcsoccer.net, this website, our Discord server and bot, and our store.

We decide how your data is used, so we're responsible for it. You can reach us at mcsoccersupport@gmail.com or on Discord.

What we collect

When you play

  • Your Minecraft username, UUID (your account's ID) and skin, which Mojang gives us when you log in.
  • Your IP address, to stop ban dodging and alt accounts and keep the server safe.
  • Your chat messages and commands, which the server keeps in its logs.
  • Everything that happens in your matches: goals, assists, passes, saves, tackles, ratings, ranked Elo and results.
  • Your settings, cosmetics, titles and Sapphires, with a history of what you've bought and spent.
  • Reports you send or receive, and staff notes on mutes and bans.

Anticheat

Our anticheat watches how you move and play to catch hacked clients. It keeps your Minecraft name and UUID, when you play, which client you use and its version (vanilla, Fabric or Lunar Client, for example) and a record of any checks you set off. It doesn't keep IP addresses. It all stays on our game server, and only staff can see it.

Chat filter

Our chat filter stars out swear words and blocks slurs: in chat, private messages, signs, books, item, club and voice group names, and what you say to Pete and Kev. A blocked message isn't saved anywhere. Staff get an alert with your name, where you typed it and when, and at most the word's first letter, and the alert also goes to a staff-only channel on our Discord. Like every command, a blocked /msg is still in the server's command log.

Voice chat

Voice chat goes through the server straight to the players near you. We don't record it. To talk, you need a linked Discord account, so staff know who's speaking.

When you link Discord

We save your Discord user ID and username with your Minecraft account. That's how we give you roles and badges and open voice chat.

On our Discord server

Our bot keeps your Discord ID and who invited you, for invite races, which also check how old your Discord account is. It keeps the reports and commands you send it too.

When you talk to Gaffer Pete or Kit Man Kev

These lobby characters are AI. What you say to them is sent to Anthropic, the company behind Claude, along with your name and public stats, so they can answer. A message with a slur is blocked before it reaches the AI. We keep a log of these chats so staff can check them. Don't tell them anything private.

When you buy something

PayNow runs the checkout and is the seller for every order, so it handles your payment details and we never see your card. PayNow tells us what was bought, which Minecraft account it's for and the order ID.

To deliver purchases, the PayNow plugin on our server also sends PayNow the IP address and UUID of each player who joins. PayNow's Privacy Policy covers how it uses them.

When you visit this site

This site has no accounts, ads, analytics or cookies. To show its pages, your browser loads fonts from Google Fonts, player skins from mc-heads.net and minotar.net, and live stats from our servers through Cloudflare. Like any website you visit, these services see your IP address. The site itself is hosted on GitHub Pages.

How we use it

  • To run matches, stats, leaderboards, ranked and tournaments.
  • To deliver what you buy and keep a record of it.
  • To moderate: handle reports, stop cheats and alt accounts, and enforce bans.
  • To give Discord roles and badges and run invite races.
  • To answer you when you contact us.
  • To fix bugs and keep everything running.

We don't sell your data, rent it out or use it for ads.

If you're in the EU or UK, the law asks us to name our reasons. We use your data to provide the service you asked for, for our legitimate interest in running a fair and safe server, and to meet legal duties like keeping payment records.

What's public

Some of MCS is meant to be seen. Your Minecraft name, skin, matches, stats, ratings, ranked Elo, club and tier list placement show on this site, through our public API, in our Discord channels and on boards in game. Anyone can see them, no account needed.

Who else gets it

A few services help us run MCS. Each one gets only what its job needs:

  • PayNow: payments, tax and receipts.
  • Anthropic: writes Pete's and Kev's replies.
  • Discord: our server, the bot and account links.
  • Folium: hosts the game server.
  • Cloudflare: runs our API and domain.
  • GitHub: hosts this website.
  • Google Fonts, mc-heads.net and minotar.net: fonts and skins on this site.
  • Mojang and Microsoft: sign you in to Minecraft.

Each has its own privacy policy. We may also share data when the law requires it, or to protect players or MCS. Most of these services are based in the United States, so your data may be handled there.

How long we keep it

  • Match records and stats: as long as MCS runs. They're the server's history.
  • Server and chat logs: as long as they're useful for moderation.
  • Chat filter alerts: the server keeps only the newest 200 staff alerts. The copy in our staff Discord channel stays until staff delete it.
  • Anticheat records: when you played and on which client for 90 days, checks you set off for a year. Your name and UUID stay as long as MCS runs.
  • Bans, and why they were given: as long as the ban lasts.
  • Purchase records: as long as tax law requires.
  • Your Discord link: until you unlink.

Your choices and rights

  • Unlink your Discord any time with /link unlink in game.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, or to correct or delete it. Email mcsoccersupport@gmail.com with your Minecraft name. We may ask you to prove the account is yours, for example by typing a code in game. We'll answer within 30 days.
  • When you ask us to delete your data, we delete or anonymize what we can. We may keep what we need for bans, fraud checks and payment records.

In the EU or UK, you can also ask us to limit or stop how we use your data or to move it to another service, and you can complain to your local data protection authority.

In California, we don't sell or share personal information for ads. We don't track you across other websites, so "Do Not Track" signals don't change anything here.

Kids

MCS is for players 13 and up, the same as Discord. If you're under 13, please don't use MCS or give us any personal information. If we learn we've collected data from a child under 13, we'll delete it. Parents can contact us any time.

Keeping it safe

Only staff can see private data like IP addresses, chat logs and reports, and our keys and passwords are kept private. No system is perfect, so if something ever goes wrong, we'll tell the players affected.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. Big changes get announced in our Discord.

Contact

Questions, or want to use any of your rights? Email us or ask us on Discord.

Connect to the server

  1. Open Minecraft: Java Edition 1.21.4 or newer and go to Multiplayer.
  2. Click Add Server and enter
  3. Join, and accept the resource pack. It carries the kits, crests and goals, so the server needs it.
  4. Walk into a pitch's ready zone and the match starts itself.
